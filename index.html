FinLedger – Personal Finance

A personal finance management application designed to help users organize, track and understand their financial activity in one place.

About FinLedger

FinLedger is a personal finance application focused on helping users keep track of their income, expenses, transactions, accounts and overall financial activity.

The application is designed with privacy and security in mind. Financial information is intended to remain under the user's control, with optional encrypted backup and restore functionality available through Google Drive.

Features

Financial Tracking

Record and organize income, expenses and other financial transactions.

Ledger

View financial activity by day, month and year to understand spending and income patterns.

Accounts

Manage financial accounts and keep track of their balances and transactions.

Financial Insights

Review financial activity and summaries to better understand personal spending and income.

SMS-based Transaction Assistance

Where permitted by the user, FinLedger can use relevant financial SMS messages to assist with transaction tracking.

Encrypted Backup

Users can optionally back up and restore their encrypted financial data using Google Drive.

Privacy & Security

FinLedger is designed to give users control over their financial information. The application does not sell personal financial information.

Google Drive backup is an optional feature used to allow users to back up and restore their FinLedger data.

Privacy Policy

Google Drive Backup

FinLedger may use Google Drive to provide optional backup and restore functionality. Backup data is encrypted by the application before it is uploaded. FinLedger requests only the access necessary for its application backup functionality.

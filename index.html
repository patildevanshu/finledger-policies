FinLedger Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

FinLedger is a personal finance application designed to help you manage transactions, accounts, credit cards, investments, and related financial information on your device.

1. Information FinLedger Handles

FinLedger may process information that you enter into the app, such as account names, balances, transactions, categories, credit-card information, investments, and app preferences. This information is used to provide the app's financial-management features.

2. SMS and Transaction Detection

If you enable SMS-based transaction detection and grant the required Android permission, FinLedger may read supported transaction messages from your device to help identify and create transactions.

SMS access is used only for providing transaction-detection functionality and is not intended for advertising or unrelated purposes.

3. Google Sign-In and Google Drive Backup

If you choose to enable Google Drive backup, FinLedger uses Google authentication and Google's app-specific Drive storage area to save your backup.

Backup data is encrypted by FinLedger before it is uploaded to Google Drive.

FinLedger uses the drive.appdata scope for app-specific Drive storage. This is intended to prevent FinLedger from accessing your normal Google Drive files.

Google Drive backup is optional. You control whether to connect your Google account to FinLedger.

4. Data Storage and Transmission

FinLedger is designed around local-first storage. Financial information is primarily stored on your device.

When you explicitly use the cloud backup feature, an encrypted backup is transmitted to Google Drive for storage.

FinLedger does not operate a separate server that collects or stores your personal financial records in its own database.

5. Data Sharing

FinLedger does not sell your personal financial information.

FinLedger does not intentionally share your financial information with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Security

FinLedger provides security features such as password/PIN protection and optional biometric unlocking, depending on your device and configuration.

Cloud backup data is encrypted by FinLedger before being uploaded to Google Drive.

While reasonable measures are used to protect your data, no software or storage system can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and Google account.

7. Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to grant SMS permissions.
  • You can choose whether to connect Google Drive backup.
  • You can use FinLedger without enabling cloud backup.
  • You can disable optional features through the app where supported.
  • You can remove FinLedger's locally stored data using Android's normal app-management controls.

8. Third-Party Services

FinLedger may interact with Google services when you choose Google authentication or Google Drive backup.

Google services are governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

9. Children's Privacy

FinLedger is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FinLedger's functionality or data practices change.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or FinLedger's data practices, please contact the FinLedger developer using the contact information provided on the official FinLedger app or developer website.
